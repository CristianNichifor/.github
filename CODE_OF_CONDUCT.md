# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience. Critique ideas and changes without harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized comments or disclosure of private information.

The maintainer may edit or remove disruptive content, close conversations, or restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to cristian@cristian-nichifor.com, with links and context. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

This applies to project spaces and to people representing these projects publicly.
